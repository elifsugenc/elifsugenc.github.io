THE EARTHEN

Concept & Premise

Likening earth’s renewal cycles to human life; cultivating physical and emotional wellbeing through tactile soil immersion, mud bathing, and clay creation.

Core Question
“To decay is to begin again — Is there life because there is death?”

Can an architectural sanctuary cultivate healing through soil immersion? Embedding rammed earth, mud baths, and ceramic kilns into the topography to reconnect the body with subterranean grounding.

Author Elifsu Genç
Institution MEF University
Studio INT 301 Studio
Year 2026
Location Elgiz Museum, İstanbul, Türkiye
Medium Soil & Clay Tectonics
Tools Rhino 3D, Enscape
01 CONCEPT & MANIFESTO — CYCLES OF EARTH • CYCLES OF LIFE
“

Earth’s natural cycles—resting, shedding, decomposing, and blooming—mirror the rhythms of human life.

In contemporary city life, exhaustion and pause are treated as flaws to be overcome. Just as soil requires fallow seasons to regain its fertility, human beings require genuine grounding and rest. By aligning with earth's natural cycles, we cultivate physical vitality and spiritual wellbeing.

ETHOS OF TACTILE GROUNDING

Healing Through Physical Immersion

Touching soil, feeling mineral mud across the skin, and shaping raw clay engage the senses directly. This physical connection grounds the mind and restores emotional balance, replacing urban burnout with a sense of renewal and presence.

Like a seed resting in dark soil before sprouting, our moments of pause are not losses—they are the quiet beginnings of new energy, resilience, and personal wellbeing.

CURATORIAL STATEMENT:

PHYSICAL & SPIRITUAL WELLBEING THROUGH EARTH

Situated amidst dense urban plazas and office blocks, The Earthen serves as an intentional sanctuary of restorative grounding. In fast-paced contemporary life, fatigue and burnout are treated as dysfunctions to be suppressed.

The Earthen offers a restorative space where visitors physically re-engage with soil, clay, and mineral mud. Through mud-bathing pools, kneading raw clay by hand, and sensory tactile immersion, visitors find emotional relief, calm, and physical vitality.

EARTH CYCLES AS METAPHORS OF LIFE RENEWAL

The architecture reflects the regenerative metabolism of the soil: resting, shedding, breaking down, and blooming anew. Just as soil recovers through natural cycles, human beings renew themselves through conscious pause and connection with nature.

Translucent skins filter daylight into a serene sanctuary, while a circulatory pipe network connects rooftop living gardens to subterranean mud baths in a continuous loop of organic healing.

TOPRAKLA GELEN FİZİKSEL VE RUHSAL ESENLİK (WELLBEING)

Yoğun iş merkezlerinin ve kentsel koşturmacanın ortasında konumlanan The Earthen, bedensel ve ruhsal iyileşme sunan bir sığınaktır. Çağdaş kent yaşamında yorgunluk ve tükenmişlik sürekli ertelenir veya bastırılır.

The Earthen, ziyaretçilerin toprak, kil ve çamurla doğrudan bedensel temas kurduğu duyusal bir ortam sağlar. Mineral çamur havuzları, kili yoğurma atölyeleri ve dokunsal yüzeyler sayesinde insan bedenine ve zihnine dinginlik ve canlılık kazandırır.

TOPRAĞIN DÖNGÜLERİNDEN YAŞAMIN DÖNGÜLERİNE

Proje, toprağın doğal döngülerini insan yaşamındaki iniş, çıkış, dinlenme ve yenilenme süreçlerine benzetir. Toprak nasıl dinlenip arınarak yeniden verimli hale gelirse, insan da durup nefes alarak tazelenir.

Yarı saydam kabuğun süzdüğü sıcak ışık altında, çatı bahçesinden zemin altı çamur havuzlarına uzanan döngüsel tesisat ağı yapıyı ve insan bedenini yaşayan ortak bir iyileşme döngüsünde buluşturur.

SEARCH & CRUSH

STAGE 02

02 ARCHITECTURAL METABOLISM // CIRCULATORY TUBE SYSTEM
SYSTEM A

THE VERTICAL CYCLE OF MATTER

The sectional organization of the project is structured upon a vertical cycle of material transformation, progressing from wet to dry, from soft to solid, and from decay to growth:

  • Lower Subterranean Level: Water-based immersion, soaking, and clay bathing allowing direct bodily engagement with liquid and semi-liquid mineral matter.
  • Ground Level Labs: Matter becomes denser and more stable, enabling kneading, shaping, and forming on radial workstations.
  • Upper Sun Terraces: Weathering and drying under natural air currents, permitting objects to cure, crack, and slowly transform.
  • +6.00m Terrace Garden: Observation and cultivation where decomposed matter creates nutrient-rich soil for sprouting plants.
SYSTEM B

HYBRID METAL TUBES: STRUCTURE & CONDUIT

The curved metal tubes extend throughout the volume, dissolving the boundary between structural architecture and interior furniture:

  • Structural Frame: Vertical radial ring providing seismic and gravity support for the central atrium and rooftop garden.
  • Functional Furniture: Tubes bend to become seating, work tables, handrails, and spatial dividers.
  • Biological Conduits: Internal hollow chambers act as vertical pipelines carrying harvested seeds, water, and clay slurry between levels, allowing the building itself to actively participate in the cycle of mixing and regeneration.
SYSTEM C

DIFFUSE TRANSLUCENT FIBERGLASS SKIN

Wrapped around the external perimeter of the tube framework, a curved fiberglass envelope modulates light and views:

  • Filters direct urban sunlight into a warm, meditative amber glow.
  • Blurs the visual threshold between the contemplative interior sanctuary and the urban context.
  • Allows natural ventilation and thermal stack effect to ventilate drying zones while preserving humidity in bathing chambers.

03 SPATIAL EXPERIENCES & ATMOSPHERES

The space offers a sequence of gentle physical activities that connect the human body with soil, clay, and planetary cycles. Visitors move through tactile encounters of kneading, grounding, and resting:

KNEADING & SHAPING LAB Earth-walled communal workshop with integrated clay bins and tactile forming stations.
