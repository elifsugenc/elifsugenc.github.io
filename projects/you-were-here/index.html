YOU WERE HERE.

Concept

When we walk through a building, our footsteps slowly wear down the floor. In digital spaces, we move just as much—scrolling, hovering, pausing—yet these movements usually feel invisible or get quietly recorded as commercial analytics. YOU WERE HERE. explores what happens when everyday digital interactions are treated as physical traces rather than hidden data.

Guiding Question
“What do we leave behind when we navigate a digital space? Can an insignificant movement become a meaningful trace?”

Faster movements leave faint, thin lines, while pauses grow into soft circles. The drawing doesn't try to prove what you felt—it simply records where your hand and attention spent time.

THE QUESTIONS

// WHAT ARE WE OBSERVING?

Almost everything we do online leaves a trace: a cursor pausing over a photo, a hesitation before clicking a link, a slow scroll down an essay. Usually, commercial platforms gather this data quietly to optimize for engagement and keep our attention locked on the screen.

CENTRAL INQUIRY:
“Who gets to collect, interpret, and own our digital traces? What happens when this information is returned to the visitor as a visual drawing?”

Instead of hiding data collection in privacy policies, YOU WERE HERE. turns the process inside out. If a visitor chooses to participate, the website visualizes their pointer movements and pauses in real-time. Each session generates a simple, anonymous drawing that can be saved, downloaded, or contributed to a collective map.

// WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT REVEAL

Approximations, Not Mind-Reading

It is important to be clear about what these drawings actually represent. Cursor movement is only a rough behavioural indicator. A pause on a screen might mean someone was reading deeply, or it might mean they looked away, took a phone call, or left to grab a cup of water. Dwell time is an approximation of presence, not proof of attention.

Participation is always voluntary. No typed text, personal identity, or sensitive information is ever recorded. You can stop participating at any time or explore the portfolio without recording anything.

YOU WERE HERE. veri toplamayı gizli bir politika olmaktan çıkarıp görünür kılar. Ziyaretçi onay verirse, sitedeki imleç hareketleri ve duraklamalar eşzamanlı olarak soyut bir çizgiye dönüşür. Her ziyaret, kaydedilip indirilebilen veya ortak bir arşive eklenebilen sade bir çizim üretir.

Bir Yaklaşım, Bir Zihin Okuma Değil

Bu çizimlerin ne anlama geldiği konusunda dürüst olmak gerekir: İmleç hareketleri dikkatin kesin bir kanıtı değil, yalnızca olası bir davranışsal göstergedir. Ekrandaki uzun bir duraklama bir metnin dikkatle okunduğunu da gösterebilir, kullanıcının o sırada çay almaya gittiğini veya ekrandan uzaklaştığını da.

HOW THE DRAWING FORMS

01 // MOVEMENT & SPEED

Lines and Velocity

Rapid cursor movements between links leave thin, light lines. Slower movements leave thicker, more deliberate paths across the page.

02 // PAUSES

Dwell Circles

When the cursor rests in one place while reading or thinking, a soft circular halo forms. The longer it lingers, the wider the circle expands.

03 // THE ARTIFACT

Personal Image (PNG)

At any point, your session's drawing can be viewed or exported as a clean 1:1 image showing the exact path your hand took while navigating.
