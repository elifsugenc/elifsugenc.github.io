YOU WERE HERE.

Concept & Premise

YOU WERE HERE. inverts the extractive surveillance mechanics of the contemporary Attention Economy. Operating as an embedded research apparatus, this portfolio website itself serves as a live field demo: recording involuntary cursor trajectories, cognitive hesitations, and reading velocities across the viewport as normalized spatial vectors.

Core Question
“What remains of our movements in the attention economy? Can digital dwell time register physical presence?”

Kinetic velocities become stroke weights, and cognitive pauses bloom into translucent halos. Every visitor co-authors this living palimpsest, returning attention data back to the public as contemplative spatial memory.

CONCEPTUAL THESIS & MANIFESTO

// THE CRITICAL APPARATUS

In an internet architecture where only deliberate actions — clicked buttons, submitted forms — are archived, the genuine weight of our digital existence is left between the lines: a cursor lingering over an essay, involuntary hesitation arcs between pages, and micro-moments of thought before action.

CENTRAL INQUIRY:
“Can an anonymous digital trajectory hold the weight of physical presence? Can digital dwell time become collective spatial memory?”

YOU WERE HERE. transforms this website into a sensitive spatial testbed. When a visitor opts in, all involuntary kinetic movements across the screen are recorded as normalized coordinate vectors. Each visit is indexed with a sequential serial number (TRACE_0001, TRACE_0002...) and etched into a permanent cloud archive. This is not a mechanism of commercial surveillance; it is a participatory digital monument where individual presence accretes into shared spatial sediment.

// RESEARCH FRAMEWORK: THE ATTENTION ECONOMY • DATA FOR WHOM AND WHY?

The Viewport as Architectural Ground: Inverting Extraction into Palimpsest

In the contemporary attention economy, digital interfaces are designed to harvest gaze, monetize dwell time, and orchestrate behavioral loops. Every pause, scroll velocity, and hesitation is captured by commercial algorithms to optimize extraction.

YOU WERE HERE. inverts this dynamic through an architectural lens. Instead of hidden telemetry, this portfolio operates as an open, transparent field demo. We ask: Where do visitors linger? What inquiries trigger cognitive pauses? How do human eyes and hands navigate when freed from commercial checkout corridors?

Here, attention data is not proprietary intelligence—it is returned to the public as collective memory. Dwell duration does not generate ad revenue; it blooms into cerulean halos of contemplative stillness. The website ceases to be a static promotional brochure and becomes an active research apparatus registering the fragile choreography of human attention.

02

CARTOGRAPHIC PILLARS

PILLAR 01 // KINETICS

Dwell Density & Velocity

Cursor movements are modulated by inverse speed calculus. Rapid transitions yield feather-light strokes, while reading hesitations bloom into translucent concentric cerulean halos, measuring cognitive attention without invading privacy.

PILLAR 02 // PALIMPSEST

Collective Layering

No trace exists in isolation. Through the collective archive stage, hundreds of past visitor trajectories are superimposed in real-time, revealing unintended migratory corridors and collective spatial desires across the website.

PILLAR 03 // ARTIFACT

Vector-to-PNG Persistence

Replacing heavy multipage documents, every trace is synthesized into a standardized, lightweight 1:1 PNG artifact. Each entry preserves date, time, duration, and coordinate geometry — readily downloadable and permanently archived.
