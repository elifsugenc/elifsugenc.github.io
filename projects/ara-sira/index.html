ARA-SIRA EDITORIAL • 2024

ARA-SIRA

Description

An investigation into dual-state spatial dynamics, collective play, and quiet contemplation within community architecture. Exploring how architecture balances the lively energy of communal encounter with the introspective silence of solitary retreat. “Can one space hold both play and quiet?”

Role

Concept development, spatial surveying, modular intervention design, public seating ergonomics, and perspective visualizations.
