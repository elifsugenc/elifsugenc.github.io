CHRONOCLINES GENERATIVE ART • 2025

CHRONOCLINES

Project Manifesto

Urban stratification and algorithmic temporal layers capturing historical city traces. Exploring generative mappings of sedimentary memory and urban change, rendering the invisible temporal palimpsest of the city tangible.

Core Question
“Can a city exhibit itself across deep time?”

By translating historical cartography, archaeological strata, and algorithmic weathering into spatial thresholds, the project visualizes the hidden layers of urban transformation.

Author Elifsu Genç
Institution MEF University
Studio Architectural Design Studio
Year 2024–2025
Location İstanbul, Türkiye
Medium Generative Mapping
Tools Rhino 3D, Grasshopper
