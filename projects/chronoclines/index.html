CHRONOCLINES ARCHITECTURAL STUDIO • 2025

CHRONOCLINES

Project Manifesto

Urban stratification and temporal incline in historic Balat. Rather than treating the archive as static storage, the museum frames history as a continuous ascending ramp — an architectural gradient where preservation, memory, and disappearance coexist.

Core Question
“Can a city exhibit itself across deep time?”

By translating historical cartography, Byzantine city walls, and multicultural craft memory into a continuous spatial incline, visitors ascend in space while descending through historical strata.

Author Elifsu Genç
Institution MEF University
Studio ARC 301 Studio
Year 2024–2025
Location Balat, İstanbul, Türkiye
Medium Museum & Urban Stratification
Tools Rhino 3D, Grasshopper, Enscape
01 URBAN STRATIFICATION & TEMPORAL INCLINE

The project proposes a museum in Balat where space itself becomes an archive of time. The core idea is a continuous ramp that gradually ascends through the building, guiding visitors on both a physical and temporal journey: with each step upward, one symbolically travels backward in history — ascending in space while descending in time. Hovering above the historic Golden Horn walls, the ground plane remains open as a living public bazaar and civic passage.
