■ ARCHIVAL PROTOCOL & VERIFICATION ETHICS

Historical records will be added only when verified.

This memorial archive operates as an independent architectural counter-monument. In respect to the dignity of victims and their families, zero speculative, simulated, or artificially generated names and casualty statistics are deployed. Records will be inducted exclusively through verified industrial health audits, court findings, and corroborated testimonies.
