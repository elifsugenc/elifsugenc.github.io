01 / QUESTIONING THE BRIEF

The project began with a brief: the encounter of two actions.

But, wait...

What is an action? When does movement become action, and does an action require intention?

03 / THE PATH

A path tells us where to go.
STRAY asks what happens when we choose not to follow it.

THE UNEXPECTED

  • getting lost
04 / STRAY

TO STRAY

to move away from the expected path.

STRAY introduces opportunities to deviate from the predetermined route, allowing unplanned encounters to generate new actions.

05 / TWO ACTIONS MEET

Design the encounter of two actions.

Rather than designing two predetermined actions, STRAY designs the conditions through which actions might encounter one another.

07 / ANOTHER QUESTION

STRAY began by asking what an action is.

Instead of defining the actions that should take place, the project began to explore how design might create conditions for actions that cannot be predicted in advance.

This left me with another question:

Can we design an action without designing what the action will be?
