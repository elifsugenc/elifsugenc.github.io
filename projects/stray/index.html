PROJECT

STRAY. drift go astray wander off roam idly lose the thread waif wander

is doing nothing doing something?

At the heart of these ideas is the human factor

The understanding that our experiences in urban spaces are personal and shaped by our interactions with them. As we wander, drift, and stray, we become active participants in the story of the city. This involvement enriches our understanding of place and fosters a sense of community.

By embracing flâner, situationism, phenomenology, psychogeography, and the Slow City movement, we can create a more meaningful urban experience, where every encounter is an opportunity for connection and discovery.
