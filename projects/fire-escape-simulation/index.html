FIRE ESCAPE SIMULATION

Concept & Premise

Agent-based computational simulation analyzing human panic dynamics, spatial bottleneck congestion, and egress optimization during high-rise fire evacuation emergencies. Testing circulation thresholds through algorithmic modeling.

Individual Role & Contributions

Design Team Lead • 3D Modeler & Level Designer: Leading the spatial design and modeling workflow; constructing the 1:1 detailed digital model of the MEF University building complex; researching empirical fire behavior and material combustion dynamics; designing spatial egress corridors, door width thresholds, and obstacle geometries for crowd evacuation simulations.

Core Question
“Can you escape the fire?”

How can computational behavior simulation guide safer architectural typologies? Modeling panic velocity, smoke spread, and exit width constraints to inform evidence-based life safety design.
