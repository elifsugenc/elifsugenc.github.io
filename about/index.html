ABOUT / ELIFSU GENÇ

About.

Elifsu Genç is an interior design and architecture student based in Istanbul/Türkiye. But she usually likes to call herself a “critical designer” instead.

Her work explores the relationships between people, materials, environments, and technology.

She is interested in how designed systems can respond to human presence, behavior, and interaction, and how materials can become active participants in these relationships.

Her interests lie in experimental and research-driven practices that bring together spatial design, material exploration, and emerging technologies.

She is currently developing her practice around interactive environments, material behavior, and human-centered design.

Words are really important for her. She likes to give meaning to every word she uses in her projects. To understand her mind you need to understand her words first.

DESIGN MANIFESTO
“I am a curious designer based in Istanbul who focuses on sustainability and the design process itself in spatial and atmospheric design. I explore the potentials of materials, produce my own materials, and integrate them into the design process. In my work, I value the relationship between existing spaces and design interventions, incorporating the memory of a space, the nature of materials, and traces of human presence into the design. I am particularly interested in biomaterials not only as sustainable alternatives, but as materials that can carry and communicate stories. Through experimenting with and producing materials myself, I explore their qualities, transformations, and imperfections, and how these can become part of the story and atmosphere of a space.”
