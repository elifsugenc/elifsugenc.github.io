MANIFESTO & PHILOSOPHY

SEE METHODOLOGY ↗
“My practice begins with overlooked experiences and the questions they raise about everyday life.”

I approach design as a way to examine the assumptions and systems shaping how people perceive, inhabit, and relate to the world. Drawing on philosophy and psychology, I begin by questioning the brief itself: whose perspectives it centers, what it leaves unspoken, and what possibilities it overlooks.

Through research, spatial design, and interactive media, I translate these inquiries into tangible experiences that invite reflection and connection. I explore biomaterials and emerging systems as tools for this work, asking how they can make hidden conditions perceptible and open new ways of seeing, understanding, and engaging with our surroundings.

MESSIER 45 — ÜLKER // THE UNSEEN STARS DEEP CLUSTER OBSERVATION • SPATIAL INQUIRY
ÜLKER ASTRONOMY × DESIGN METHODOLOGY

THE UNSEEN STARS OF ÜLKER

“To the naked eye, the Ülker cluster appears as only six or seven prominent stars. But my projects are not the stars everyone already sees: I looked into the dark spaces between them and saw the invisible stars, and those unseen stars became my projects.”

01 // BEYOND THE VISIBLE STARS (GÖRÜNENİN ÖTESİ)

OBSERVING WHAT IS NOT YET VISIBLE

Human gaze has traditionally looked at the Ülker (Pleiades) cluster to count its six or seven bright sisters. But in astronomy, the cluster is an immense family of over a thousand stars and cold brown dwarfs hidden within interstellar reflection dust. Conventional architecture similarly focuses on the obvious, loud, and already visible landmarks. My inquiries deliberately step into the dark open spaces between the famous stars to observe what remained unseen.

02 // INVISIBLE REALITIES AS ARCHITECTURE

THE PROJECTS AS UNSEEN STARS

Each project in this portfolio gives form to an unseen presence: invisible civic labor spatialized into memory (Emek Museum), silent non-human urban rhythms (Stray), the delicate decay of biopolymers returning to earth (Bio-Decay), panic thresholds in evacuation (Fire Escape Simulation), and subterranean soil regeneration (The Earthen). They are not monuments of certainty, but questions that illuminate what was hidden in plain sight.
