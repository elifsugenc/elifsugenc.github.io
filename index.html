METHODOLOGY — SPATIAL INQUIRY & DESIGN FRAMEWORK
• PRACTICE FRAMEWORK / 3 MODES OF SPATIAL INQUIRY / CRITICAL & EXPERIMENTAL

METHODOLOGY AS SPATIAL INQUIRY

“Architecture is not about delivering predetermined answers. It is an investigative tool to question what is taken for granted, make the invisible tangible, and open spaces for reflection.”

I approach design as an open inquiry into the systems, assumptions, and environments shaping how we inhabit space. Rather than executing closed briefs, my practice operates as a continuous dialogue: deconstructing institutional norms, unearthing overlooked site memories, and prototyping living, weathering materials that return questions back to the public.

01

THE DESIGN PRACTICE // 3 MODES OF INQUIRY

All 12 projects in the portfolio develop through three interconnected modes of inquiry—moving from deconstruction, to site excavation, to tactile material prototyping:

PHASE 01 // CRITICAL INQUIRY

QUESTION THE GIVEN

“Whose perspectives are centered, and what is left unspoken?”

Spatial briefs are not neutral instructions. Projects begin by interrogating institutional assumptions: who benefits, whose labor built the space, and whose presence is erased. Design becomes an investigative tool to dismantle what is taken for granted.

KEY PROJECTS:
01 • Emek Museum ↗ 12 • Fire Escape Sim ↗ 02 • Stray ↗
PHASE 02 // CONTEXTUAL RESEARCH

REVEAL THE UNSEEN

“What does matter, site, and everyday life remember?”

Context is treated not as an empty plot, but as an unwritten physical archive. The practice excavates conditions conventional architecture ignores: invisible municipal labor, street animals, passive microclimatic shadows, and sedimentary site histories.

KEY PROJECTS:
04 • Chronoclines ↗ 07 • Bajo la Fresca ↗ 05 • Millieu ↗ 09 • Ara Sıra ↗
PHASE 03 // EXPERIMENTAL THINKING

TEST THROUGH MATTER

“How does space breathe, age, and invite reflection?”

Materials are not static decorative surfaces; they possess agency, age, breathe, and decompose. Questions achieve resonance when tested through physical touch: raw adobe immersion, circular biopolymers, tactile timber joinery, and interactive digital traces.

KEY PROJECTS:
10 • The Earthen ↗ 11 • Bio-Decay ↗ 08 • Wood Weave ↗ 06 • Sprouting Garden ↗ Demo • You Were Here ↗

A CONTINUOUS INQUIRY LOOP // PRACTICE SYNTHESIS

THE PROJECT AS A QUESTION, NOT AN ANSWER

Architecture does not shut down dialogue with a definitive conclusion. Every spatial installation, prototype, or drawing cycles back to the public—sharpening curiosity, awakening forgotten connections, and keeping the inquiry alive.

02

CORE PRINCIPLES // ETHICAL STANCE

01

THE QUESTION IS THE OUTCOME

A project does not shut down dialogue with a definitive answer; it sharpens the inquiry and equips visitors with new ways of seeing the ordinary.

02

MATTER HAS LIVING AGENCY

Materials are not static, disposable surfaces. They respire, conduct heat, weather, and decompose. Designing with materiality means designing with time.

03

MULTISPECIES & CIVIC JUSTICE

Architecture must shelter overlooked laborers, stray organisms, and fragile ecologies. A design is judged by how generously it shares room with the vulnerable.

04

TANGIBLE SPECULATION

Philosophical and political questions achieve resonance only when tested through physical touch, sensory thresholds, and rigorous prototypes.

01 // ÜLKER & THE SEVENTH SISTER

THE THRESHOLD OF VISION

While only six or seven stars of the Pleiades are typically visible to the naked eye, the cluster actually contains over a thousand stars. In the Ülker cluster (Pleiades), Merope is the hidden seventh sister—barely visible to the naked eye. In mythology, she dimmed her light; in astronomy, she marks the threshold of human perception: what exists, but is easily overlooked in plain sight.

02 // METHODOLOGY AS REVELATION

THE PRACTICE CORE

This defines Elifsu’s methodology: instead of loud monumentalism, projects investigate hidden spatial layers—invisible civic labor, non-human agency, fragile earthen cycles, and silent digital traces. In the constellation, this star morphs into the central Design Approach.
